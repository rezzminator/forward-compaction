# A run, as expected

Each step names what an audit of the transcript and the plugin's log rows sees.

1. Below its first nudge level the party works undisturbed: each Claude Code ask logs `compact` with decision `skip`, why `waiting`, and nothing reaches the model but main's finish notice (step 2).
2. At each nudge level the model reads one nudge with a tool result, logged `nudge` with its level, its `notice` count and `last`; no level nudges twice, and no two nudges between installs read alike. Main at its finish notice point (`finishReached`) reads the finish notice once before its first nudge, logged `finish-notice`.
3. The model finishes its step, then calls `forward_compaction` with only its changes, removing what is finished, lifted or wrong and editing what changed: the result gives one line per change with its item numbers, an item that left (removed, or the text an edit replaced) shown by its first `LEFT_SHOWN_CHARS` characters, its whole text in the list's history file, a note per skipped entry, the frontier's size, with `done` split `Done: {S} newest {item stays|items stay} in your wake, {N} earlier in {path}`, then `Frontier stored`, or below the notice point `Frontier updated`, and a `stored` row records `compacts`, its `lists`, `frontier`, `done` (`{shown, shownChars, earlier, earlierChars}`), `storage` and `files`. A frontier without `objective` or `next` gets `, without {objective|next}: send it before it installs, …`, its `stored` and installing `compact` rows `incomplete`; a waiting call's `stored` row has `waits`.
4. The next ask installs a stored call that compacts (main also at its turn end; after one that does not, asks stay as in step 1; a waiting one first logs `skip`, why `unfinished`): `compact` with decision `install`, why `stored`, then `landed` with `before` above `after` and the cache `read` and `written` of the first request after it; the transcript holds one compact boundary per install.
5. The wake is the facts message (`[forward-compaction: facts]`), the frontier message (`[forward-compaction: frontier]`, the frontier rendered from its lists), then the kept tail. A sub-agent's wake opens with its start (`keptStart`): the user messages its transcript opens with (its brief, with what Claude Code attached) handed back untouched by handle, then the fixed assistant line `START_END`, so each later compaction reads that prefix from the prompt cache; its facts head points to the brief above in place of `## Your brief`, and the `wake` debug row's `start` counts the messages above the facts and its `briefFiles` names the documents picked. The facts run in this order, an empty section left out or saying why it is empty: standing orders; messages arrived after the frontier; plan mode and plan file; the brief and the documents it names (sub-agent) or the user's messages (main); notifications or delivered messages; results of the calls beside the frontier; skills in force and skills not carried; files touched; shell commands run; tasks; agents; background shells and crons; the `load_files` carried; the storage; the files not carried; on a mechanical wake, the files being worked on. Outside Claude Code's expanded view (ctrl+o, `--verbose`, an export) `wakeRow` folds these hook-built rows (origin `unclassified`, or `coordinator` in a sub-agent's view; the user's typing never folds): the facts message draws nothing, the message after it (the frontier, or a mechanical wake's no-frontier message) one dim line naming ctrl+o; the stored messages the model reads stay whole.
6. Every fact is true of the woken party: nothing another party read, loaded, ran or started appears as its own, and a fact the plugin cannot tie to a party says so.
7. After the wake the party continues from its `next`, obeys every standing order and ruling, redoes nothing its `done` lists, and finishes its task correctly.
8. The run writes no `errors.jsonl` or `warnings.jsonl` row for the party, and every number in a nudge, receipt or wake has its API source (`../CLAUDE.md` § Rules).
9. Under `FORWARD_COMPACTION_MAIN_FORK`, a `/compact` typed in an idle main chat logs a `fork-prompt` debug row, a `fork` row with `trigger` `manual`, `outcome` `applied`, `ms`, `effort` and `usage`, a `stored` row with `writer: "fork"`, then `compact` with decision `install`, why `fork`, and `landed`; the kept tail starts at main's last assistant message. A failed fork logs its `outcome` as an error, the one screen line, and a `compact` row with decision `native` carrying `fork:`; Claude Code's own summary runs. Nudges and the finish notice go out as with the option off.
10. A `forward_compaction` call carrying `model`, made only on the user's ask, logs a `model-switch` debug row with `phase` `tried`, `outcome` `answered` and its `ms`, then `pending` with its `asked`, `at` (`request` below its notice point, `compaction` otherwise) and `from`; a `compaction` one logs `armed` at main's next compaction. From the request it is due, main's `turn.step` debug rows keep `model` as Claude Code named it, and `usageModel`, the response's `usage.model`, is the proof: a `usageModel` other than `model` is the switch answering, beside a `model-switch` `step` row with `given`, `sent` and `answered`. The first such answer logs `took` and says one screen line; a sub-agent's `turn.step` rows show its own model. A name its trial did not answer logs `tried` with its `outcome`, `why` and `ms`, says one warning line and logs no `pending`; main's requests keep the model Claude Code names. `/model`, a relaunch or Claude Code's fallback to another model logs `ended` with `reason` `model-changed`, a name the trial passed that Claude Code does not take (an alias of main's model) `ended` with `not-taken` and a warning line; main's `model-switch.json` holds the switch, `{}` once it ended.
